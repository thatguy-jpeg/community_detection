The Community Detection Lab page is published. It has a Girvan–Newman view and a Spectral view, with a toggle at the top.

**Girvan–Newman view**
- It starts on a fake network of 15–20 people in 3 groups, joined by 5 bridge edges. The "New graph" button builds a different one.
- Edge thickness and darkness show betweenness. The next edge to remove is highlighted.
- Pressing "Remove highest-betweenness edge" recomputes betweenness and recolors the nodes by component. "Next split" removes edges until the graph splits, and "Run" auto-plays.
- A modularity Q chart updates after every split and marks the best k. A "Show the full curve" box reveals the whole curve in advance. A table shows each community's term in Q.

**Spectral view**
- It uses the 6-node graph from the lecture. A stepper highlights A, D, L = D − A, the eigenvalues, and the sign split. The Fiedler vector is plotted per node, and nodes are colored by sign.
- You can click two nodes to add or remove an edge and watch everything recompute.

**What I verified** (in Node, not in a browser):
- **Betweenness and spectral:** a hand-checkable path graph gave the right betweenness. The lecture graph gave eigenvalues 0, 1, 3, 3, 4, 5, and a Fiedler vector of ±0.29, ±0.58 with {1,2,3} vs {4,5,6}. Both match your slides.
- **Q peak:** my first generator failed on 120 of 5,000 test graphs, either because Q peaked at the wrong k or because the best-Q split didn't match the groups. I made the groups denser and added a check inside "New graph". It only keeps graphs where Q peaks at k = 3 with a clean margin and the split matches the built groups. After that, 0 of 5,000 tested seeds failed. The smallest gap between Q at k = 3 and the runner-up was 0.05.
- **Page behavior:** I clicked through every control in a simulated browser and got no script errors.
